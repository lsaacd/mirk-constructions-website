# Project Rules for Mirk Construction Website

## Copywriting & Technical Terminology Guidelines
- **Professional General Contractor Terminology**: Always format and refine website copy into standardized, high-end general contracting terms.
- **Scope Clarity**: Ensure client-facing copy clearly delineates core specializations (e.g., Kitchen & Bath Remodels, Whole-Home Renovations), trade strengths (e.g., Electrical & Plumbing), and explicit service exclusions (e.g., Carpet, Garage Doors, Gutters, HVAC, Fans, Metal Fencing) so potential clients understand exactly what services are provided.
- **Paid Consultations/Estimates**: Never advertise estimates or in-person consultations as 'free', as in-person contractor consultations are charged.
